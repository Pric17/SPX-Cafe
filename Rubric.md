## Professional Portfolio Presentation — /10 pts

**Fully Professional Portfolio** — *10 to >8 pts*
Student produces a consistently and clearly formatted portfolio document. >> Document will include an appropriate Cover Page, Table Contents and each Section clearly identified using Headings which will appear in the Table of Contents with appropriate page numbers. All Pages will include page headers and page footers identifying the project, author, date, page numbers etc., and appropriate fonts and font sizes. All Images will be consistently and appropriately sized, a good quality and resolution - not pixellated and appropriate brightness - and text is readable to the naked eye.

**Mostly Professional Portfolio** — *8 to >4 pts*
Student produces a mostly consistent and clearly formatted portfolio document. >> Document will include many of the following features. Including an appropriate Cover Page, Table Contents and each Section clearly identified using Headings which will appear in the Table of Contents with appropriate page numbers. Most Pages will include page headers and page footers identifying the project, author, date, page numbers etc., and mostly appropriate fonts and font sizes. Most Images will be consistently and appropriately sized, a good quality and resolution - not pixellated and appropriate brightness - and most text in the images is readable to the naked eye.

Student produces a somewhat consistently and clearly formatted portfolio document. >> Document may include some of the following elements - an appropriate Cover Page, Table Contents and each Section may be clearly identified using Headings which will appear in the Table of Contents with appropriate page numbers. Some Pages will include page headers and page footers identifying the project, author, date, page numbers etc., and appropriate fonts and font sizes. Some Images will be consistently and appropriately sized, a somewhat good quality and resolution - not pixellated and appropriate brightness - and some text is readable to the naked eye.

Pages do not include page headers and page footers identifying the project, author, date, page numbers etc., and inappropriate fonts and font sizes. Images are not consistently and appropriately sized, not a good quality and resolution - may or may not be pixellated and inappropriate brightness - and text may not be readable to the naked eye.
